Ph.D. Applicant · Fall 2027

Engineering cardiac tissues to understand how identity becomes function.

I'm Young Hyun Kim, a bioengineering researcher at Sejong University. I engineer chamber-specific human cardiac organoids from hiPSCs and use quantitative phenotyping to connect cellular identity with calcium dynamics, electrical propagation, and contraction.

I am especially interested in how mechanical cues and tissue context reshape these electromechanical phenotypes.

Build the model. Measure the function. Ask why it changes.
