Ph.D. Applicant · Fall 2027

Cardiac organoids. Calcium dynamics. Quantitative tools.

I'm Young Hyun Kim, a research assistant in the Biological Systems Engineering Laboratory at Sejong University. I build human cardiac organoid models and develop tools to measure calcium dynamics and tissue motion.

I developed iCAMAnalyzer, an integrated Python platform for calcium and motion analysis, and validated calcium-derived propagation measurements using synthetic videos. I am now developing synchronized calcium–brightfield imaging to examine how calcium dynamics relate to contraction within the same beat.
