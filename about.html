Bioengineer · Maker · Researcher

About Me

I'm Young Hyun Kim, a research assistant in the Biological Systems Engineering Laboratory at Sejong University. I earned my M.S. in Integrative Bioscience and Biotechnology at Sejong in August 2026. Since September 2026, I have been developing imaging and stimulation systems for cardiac organoids as a research assistant in the lab.

I study how engineered cardiac tissues beat. My work includes calcium imaging, motion analysis, and microelectrode array (MEA) recording. I want to understand how electrical activity, calcium signals, and contraction relate within a single beat.

Build a system. Measure how it behaves. Ask a better question.

My path

Turning Points

From building robots to engineering living systems.

  1. 2017–2019 · Robotics & education

    Learning by making.

    At CreArtBot, I built interactive robots and taught robotics and STEAM workshops. Turning an idea into something that moved taught me to build, test, and explain a system.

  2. Oct 2022–Aug 2023 · Undergraduate research assistant

    From machines to living systems.

    As one of the lab’s first three members, I established its initial protocol for making cardiac organoids from human iPSCs. I also built 3D skeletal muscle tissues from C2C12 cells and applied cyclic stretch to study maturation. I earned my B.Eng. in Integrative Bioscience and Biotechnology at Sejong in August 2023.

    See the cardiac models →
  3. Sep 2023–Aug 2026 · Graduate research assistant

    Building the tissue and the tools.

    For my master’s thesis, I generated atrial- and ventricular-like cardiac organoids and developed iCAMAnalyzer to compare their calcium signals and contractile motion.

    Explore the analysis platform →
