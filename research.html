Research

Cardiac Tissue Engineering & Computational Bioimaging

I work on human iPSC-derived chamber-specific cardiac organoids — ventricular and atrial — focusing on tissue maturation, disease modeling, and quantitative analysis of calcium-handling and electromechanical function. Alongside the wet-lab work, I build the custom computational pipelines and instrumentation needed to characterize cardiac microtissue at scale.

Master's Thesis

My graduate thesis

Generation of Chamber-Specific 3D Cardiac Organoids and an Integrated Electro-Mechanical Optical Analysis Platform

M.S. in Integrative Bioscience & Biotechnology · Sejong University · August 2026 · Advisor: Keel Yong Lee, Ph.D.

My master's thesis built two things at once: chamber-specific 3D cardiac organoids — separate atrial and ventricular tissues from human iPSCs via timed retinoic-acid signaling — and iCAMAnalyzer, an integrated platform that reads their calcium kinetics, conduction, and mechanical contraction from the same tissue. Together they give each chamber a quantitative "functional fingerprint," validated with chamber-selective drugs and scaled toward high-throughput screening.

What the thesis delivers

1
Chamber-specific 3D organoids

A retinoic-acid–timed biphasic Wnt protocol reproducibly yields distinct atrial (aCO) and ventricular (vCO) organoids, confirmed by molecular markers.

2
The iCAMAnalyzer platform

One interface reads Ca²⁺ transient kinetics, spatiotemporal activation mapping, and label-free contractility from the same tissue — activation mapping validated to R² > 0.99 against synthetic ground truth.

3
Pharmacological validation

Chamber-selective drug responses (e.g. carbachol and isoproterenol) confirm the organoids' physiological fidelity.

4
High-throughput scalability

Shaker-based self-assembly and a 3D-printed micro-pillar array enable parallel analysis of nine organoids at once.

Experience

Research Experience

Sep 2023 – Present

Graduate Research Assistant — Biological Systems Engineering Laboratory

Dept. of Integrative Bioscience & Technology, Sejong University, Seoul, Korea · PI: Prof. Keel Yong Lee

Thesis project — Chamber-specific cardiac organoid platform

  • Fabrication: Differentiated hiPSCs into chamber-specific (ventricular and atrial) cardiomyocytes via temporal dual modulation of canonical Wnt signaling, using retinoic acid (1 µM) to direct atrial vs. ventricular specification, and fabricated 2D/3D cardiac organoids — achieving ~97.5% cTNT⁺ ventricular purity by flow cytometry.
  • Structural characterization: Established a chamber-identity workflow combining qRT-PCR (NR2F2, IRX4), immunofluorescence, and flow cytometry (TNNT2); built a MATLAB Organoid Diameter Analyzer validated against manual measurement by Bland–Altman analysis.
  • Functional characterization: Designed, built, and validated iCAMAnalyzer, a unified Python-based tool integrating calcium-imaging and motion-tracking analysis of cardiac organoids (ΔF/F₀ normalization, BPM, amplitude, CTD60/90, τ, Vₘₐₓ/Vᵣₑₗₐₓ, activation mapping, conduction-velocity estimation, contraction magnitude/duration) — achieving ~7% error against Python-simulated ground-truth data, with support for simultaneous multi-organoid (9-well) imaging and automated ROI detection.

Additional projects & lab responsibilities

  • Currently developing a spinner-flask (stirred-suspension) bioreactor protocol to scale up hiPSC-cardiomyocyte production, supporting higher-throughput organoid fabrication. (in progress)
  • Engineered 3D skeletal muscle tissue on custom, 3D-printed micropillar scaffolds — designed via COMSOL Multiphysics simulations of microfluidic fluid velocity and electric-potential fields — and assessed contractile function and calcium-signal propagation.
  • Provided hiPSC-derived cardiomyocytes as a shared resource to an inter-lab collaboration with Dr. Chun Jeih Ryu's cancer research group, supporting a co-authored study on CD147/CD98hc-targeted hepatocellular carcinoma therapy. → See publication
  • Manage ongoing lab operations — budget/purchasing, reagent and inventory tracking, new-member onboarding, and lab meeting scheduling.
