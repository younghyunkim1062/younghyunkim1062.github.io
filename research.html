Research

Cardiac Tissue Engineering & Computational Bioimaging

I work on human iPSC-derived chamber-specific cardiac organoids — ventricular and atrial — focusing on tissue maturation, disease modeling, and quantitative analysis of calcium-handling and electromechanical function. Alongside the wet-lab work, I build the custom computational pipelines and instrumentation needed to characterize cardiac microtissue at scale.

Research interests

Areas I work in — and dabble in

The core is chamber-specific cardiac organoids and the tools to measure them; I've explored around the edges too.

Chamber-specific cardiac organoids

hiPSC-derived atrial and ventricular 3D tissues — differentiation, maturation, and chamber-identity characterization.

Computational cardiac analysis

Calcium-transient kinetics, spatiotemporal activation mapping, and label-free contractility — via iCAMAnalyzer and custom pipelines.

Disease modeling & cardiotoxicity

Chamber-selective pharmacology and functional "fingerprints" for precision drug-screening and disease modeling.

Bioinstrumentation & fabrication

3D-printed micro-pillar arrays, field-pacing platforms, and a bio-synchronous electromechanical stimulator.

Muscle tissue & biohybrid actuators

Engineered skeletal-muscle tissue and muscle-powered robotics — an interest that grew from a review I co-authored.

Scientific software

Analysis tools, multi-organoid imaging pipelines, and small web apps that make lab work reproducible.
