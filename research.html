My work so far

Build + Measure

My M.S. research focused on the two central experimental stages of this framework: engineering chamber-specific human cardiac models, and developing quantitative methods to evaluate their function.

M.S. in Integrative Bioscience & Biotechnology · Sejong University · August 2026 · Advisor: Keel Yong Lee, Ph.D.

02 — Build: Chamber-Specific Cardiac Organoids

hiPSC · Wnt / RA modulation · 3D biphasic protocol

Model generation. hiPSC → Wnt modulation → RA specification → atrial / ventricular 3D organoids.

Validation. NR2F2 / IRX4 · MLC2a/v · Cx40/43 · sarcomeric organization.

03 — Measure: Integrated Electro-Mechanical Phenotyping

Ca²⁺ kinetics · Activation mapping · Motion analysis

Excitation→Ca²⁺→Contraction

Electrical / Ca²⁺ — iCAMAnalyzer

  • Ca²⁺ transient kinetics
  • Activation mapping
  • Conduction velocity
Same tissue → integrated E–C phenotype

Mechanical — Motion Analysis

Brightfield motion-tracking pipeline (Python)
  • Beating rate
  • Contraction magnitude
  • Contraction duration
Validation

Physiological Validation

Carbachol · Isoproterenol — chamber-selective drug responses confirming the organoids behave like the chambers they model.

Application

Scalability

9-organoid parallel acquisition — a proof-of-concept for moving this platform toward higher-throughput screening.

Other engineering

Other Engineering Projects

Instrumentation and fabrication work alongside the main organoid platform.

Bio-Synchronous Electromechanical Stimulator

A custom-built stimulator that paces 3D cardiac tissue in sync with its own contraction signal.

3D Skeletal Muscle Tissue Engineering

Engineered skeletal-muscle constructs — a second tissue system alongside the cardiac work.

Micropillar Culture Platform

A 3D-printed micropillar platform for multi-organoid culture and imaging, designed around the scalability work above.
