Current work · Hardware in development

Connecting calcium
with contraction.

I am developing a dual-camera microscope for synchronized fluorescence and brightfield imaging. The goal is to resolve the relationship between calcium dynamics and tissue motion within the same beat.

Optical integration and timing validation are ongoing.
