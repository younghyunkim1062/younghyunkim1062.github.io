Flow cytometry

A representative cTnT analysis from monolayer-derived cells.

＋Gating sequence + control + summary
Materials to add
  • An exported gating sequence and the control used to define the positive population.

Imaging & Functional Measurements

My functional-imaging work connects fluorescence and brightfield recordings with region-based signal extraction and quantitative analysis.

Skills in practice · Fluo-4 imaging · Calcium activation mapping · Brightfield motion analysis · ImageJ / Python / MATLAB

M3AEvidence to be added

Calcium imaging

An acquisition-to-analysis example using a real fluorescence recording.

＋Source video → ROI → calcium trace
Materials to add
  • One representative Fluo-4 recording, the selected ROI, and its extracted calcium trace.
  • Frame rate, exposure, pixel calibration, differentiation day, and pacing or drug conditions where applicable.
  • Explain the background/baseline treatment, checks for bleaching or motion artifacts, and personal acquisition and analysis roles.
M3BEvidence to be added

Brightfield motion analysis

A representative tissue-motion workflow with inspectable tracking and outputs.

＋Source video → tracking → motion trace
Materials to add
  • One brightfield recording, an ROI or tracking overlay, and the corresponding signal and exported results.
  • Frame rate, pixel calibration, tissue identity, and recording conditions.
  • Show how tissue motion was distinguished from stage drift or tracking failure. Define the reported signal and units, and state personal responsibilities.

Calcium and brightfield examples represent separate acquisitions unless a synchronized recording is explicitly identified. Image-derived motion is reported with its signal definition and calibration.
