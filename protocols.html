Methods & Workflows

Chamber-Specific Cardiomyocyte Differentiation

hiPSCs are cultured in 2D before embryoid-body formation and subsequent cardiac differentiation in 3D. Wnt and retinoic-acid modulation support atrial- and ventricular-like tissue generation. Characterization includes qRT-PCR and flow cytometry in monolayer cultures, alongside immunofluorescence in 3D tissues.

iCAMAnalyzer — Integrated Python Analysis Platform

An integrated Python platform combining calcium-transient analysis, calcium-based activation mapping, and label-free motion analysis. The calcium modules support ΔF/F₀ normalization, ensemble-averaged kinetics, beat-to-beat analysis, pixelwise activation timing, and calcium-derived propagation velocity estimation.

Validation used synthetic videos with known propagation velocities, including assessment of frame rate, noise, and spatial resolution, plus frame-by-frame checks against experimental recordings. Calcium-derived propagation velocity is not a direct measurement of electrical conduction velocity.

Parallel Multi-Organoid Analysis

The Python analysis workflow supports parallel analysis across multiple organoids, including a nine-organoid proof of concept, with automated ROI detection and manual correction. Calcium and brightfield recordings are analyzed as separate acquisitions; parallel analysis refers to multiple organoids, not simultaneous calcium–brightfield imaging.

Synchronized calcium–brightfield acquisition is a separate dual-camera hardware project currently under development.

Organoid Diameter Analyzer

Custom MATLAB tool for automated organoid diameter measurement from brightfield images, cross-validated against manual measurement.

Validated via Bland–Altman and correlation analysis (GraphPad Prism).

Fabrication of 3D skeletal muscle tissue on custom scaffolds, with assessment of contractile function and calcium-signal propagation.
