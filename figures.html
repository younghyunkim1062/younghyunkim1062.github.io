Scientific Illustration & Visual Communication

I translate complex biological and engineering concepts into clear visual frameworks. I develop the scientific structure, composition, and final artwork for my own research figures, from raw experimental data to graphical abstracts and publication-ready schematics. I create these illustrations using Adobe Illustrator and Inkscape.
