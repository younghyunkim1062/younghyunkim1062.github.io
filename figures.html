Figures & Scientific Illustration

I design the figures for my own papers — turning raw data (calcium traces, flow cytometry plots, organoid images) into graphical abstracts and publication figures. It's a skill that grew directly out of needing to explain my own experiments clearly, and it's become one of the things I most enjoy about doing research. More sketches and illustration work over on Instagram — @yh.bioatelier.
